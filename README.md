# MTXGovBenefits

Product-focused landing-page prototype for MTXGovBenefits, a configurable integrated eligibility product for health and human services agencies.

## Product story

The page presents:

- A shared household journey across benefit discovery, application, verification, determination, delivery and renewal
- Industry pain points paired with specific MTX product responses
- Twelve configurable product capabilities
- Role-based experiences for residents, workers, partners and program leaders
- Recommended product measures with a clearly labeled illustrative dashboard
- Modular architecture and phased adoption model

## Data note

The national figures are public program context and are not MTX results. The analytics dashboard uses example values to demonstrate the product experience; agencies establish baselines, targets and outcomes during implementation.
